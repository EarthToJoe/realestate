Erase the {{ p.role }}'s personal data?

This erases the {{ p.role }}'s name, e-mail, phone, address and any financial details. Documents never stored names, so they will show "[erased]". {{ retention }} The erase is recorded in the audit log.

A rule to weigh

New York requires a brokerage to keep its transaction records for three years.

Why

Listing and buyer agreements, contracts, disclosures and their signed acknowledgments must be kept three years (19 NYCRR 175.23; RPL §443; L17). In production, a request inside that period would erase marketing and other data at once and the signed records once the period ends. This demonstration erases on request to show that it works.
