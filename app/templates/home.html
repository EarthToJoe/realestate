How to read the screens

AI Blue is the AI at work: what it wrote, sent or worked out.
Client decision Amber is a decision only the seller or the buyer can make: you press it for them.
NY law today Red is where New York law today requires a licensed broker or a lawyer.
A person Grey is a step a person or another firm does: a notary, an inspector, the owner at the door.

Presenting to someone? "I'm presenting" in the menu (top right) adds the presenter's notes, "Do this screen for me" and skip ahead.
