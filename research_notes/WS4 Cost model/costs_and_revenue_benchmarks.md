# WS4 Cost model: costs and revenue benchmarks for a one-broker, software-driven residential brokerage in New York (Hudson Valley start)

Research date: 2026-10-23. All prices are as fetched on that date unless a source date is given. Labels: [PRIMARY] = official fee schedule, statute/regulation, vendor's own pricing page, MLS/association page or market report; [SECONDARY] = reputable third party summarising a primary; [CLAIMED] = vendor marketing, competitor comparison or aggregator figure not independently verified; [INFERENCE] = my own calculation or estimate from cited inputs. Anything sourced before 2024 is flagged "pre-2024". Two method caveats: (1) the shared web-search quota ran out part-way through, so some items rest on direct page fetches only; (2) several vendor pages (Redfin, Prevu, Zillow, Dotloop, Matterport, Google Workspace, NYSAR, tax.ny.gov's RP-5217 page) block automated fetching, so those figures are secondary or marked as gaps.

## 1. Licensing and entity: NY DOS broker licence, attorney applicant, LLC formation, publication, assumed name, escrow

### Takeaway
A NY broker licence costs $185 per two-year term (same for the individual, the LLC/corporation, and each member/officer who will act as broker), with $185 per branch office; an LLC costs $200 to form plus roughly $425–$685 all-in for the mandatory six-week newspaper publication in the Hudson Valley counties (vendor prices), $25 for an assumed name and $9 every two years. The licensing article does not apply to attorneys at all (RPL §442-f), and attorneys are exempt from continuing education, but I found no DOS page stating the commonly cited exam/education exemption for attorney broker applicants; the attorney should confirm with DOS (attorney-status applications are paper-only).

### Cited Findings
- Broker licence fees: "$185 - Initial Application", "$185 - Renewal", "$185 - Branch Office Application", "$15 - Written Exam"; applicants must be 20, have 2 years as a licensed salesperson or 3 years general real-estate experience (points system), 152 hours of qualifying education and pass a 2½-hour exam; a passed exam is valid two years; Property Condition Disclosure Statement "REQUIRED BEGINNING JULY 1, 2025". [PRIMARY, DOS, fetched 2026-10-23] — [NY DOS: Become a Real Estate Broker](https://dos.ny.gov/become-real-estate-broker)
- "Term of licensure is two years." [PRIMARY] — [NY DOS: Real Estate Broker](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-broker); renewal reminders go out three months before expiry; "A Real Estate Broker license is good for two years." — [NY DOS: Renew or Update](https://dos.ny.gov/renew-or-update-real-estate-broker-license)
- Statutory fee basis: broker licence "one hundred fifty-five dollars plus an additional thirty dollar surcharge" (= $185); salesperson $55 + $10; a corporation/LLC/partnership broker licence costs the same as an individual broker licence, and "Each additional officer, member, or manager who will act as a broker needs a separate license expiring on the same date as the entity's license. The fee for each is the same." Licence term "not less than two years" (staggered, prorated). [PRIMARY, statute] — [RPL §441-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/441-B)
- Entity licensing: "Any person, copartnership, limited liability company or corporation desiring to act as a real estate broker" must apply; the LLC application lists "the name of the company, and the name and residence of each of its members"; fees "shall not be refundable". [PRIMARY] — [RPL §441](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/441)
- Attorneys: Article 12-A "shall not apply to receivers, referees, administrators, executors, guardians or other persons appointed by or acting under the judgment or order of any court; or public officers while performing their official duties, or attorneys at law" (no qualifier on the attorney clause; text last revised 2014-09-22). [PRIMARY, statute] — [RPL §442-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/442-F)
- DOS FAQ: "all real estate licensees, with the exception of Attorneys admitted to the New York State Bar, must complete 22.5 hours of continuing education"; "If you are applying based on attorney status ... please submit a paper application by regular mail"; "A real estate broker is responsible for the supervision and conduct of the real estate brokerage business"; change of association $20; duplicate licence/pocket card $10; principal broker address change $10 per transaction; a broker must "terminate a licensee within five days from the date they leave the broker's employment". [PRIMARY] — [NY DOS Broker FAQ](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-broker-frequently-asked-questions)
- Escrow: "A real estate broker shall not commingle the money or other property of his principal with his own and shall at all times maintain a separate, special bank account to be used exclusively for the deposit of said monies ... which deposit shall be made within three business days"; funds only in "a federally insured bank account"; interest not retained by the broker except applied to earned commission with all parties' consent. [PRIMARY, regulation] — [19 NYCRR 175.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/19-NYCRR-175.1)
- LLC formation: "The fee for filing the Articles of Organization is $200"; LLC Law §206 requires the articles or a notice to be "published in two newspapers for six consecutive weeks" designated by the county clerk; Certificate of Publication filed with a "$50 filing fee"; expedited handling and certified copies cost extra (amounts not on page). [PRIMARY] — [NY DOS: Forming an LLC](https://dos.ny.gov/forming-limited-liability-company-new-york)
- Biennial Statement: "The fee for filing a Biennial Statement for a business corporation or LLC is $9", due in the formation anniversary month every two years, filed online. [PRIMARY] — [NY DOS: Biennial Statements](https://dos.ny.gov/biennial-statements-business-corporations-and-limited-liability-companies)
- Assumed name (DBA): "FEE: Limited Liability Companies and Limited Partnerships - $25. Corporations - $25 plus the fee for each county ... $100 additional [NYC counties] ... $25 [other counties]". [PRIMARY, DOS form] — [NY DOS Certificate of Assumed Name (GBL §130)](https://dos.ny.gov/certificate-assumed-name-domestic-and-foreign-limited-liability-companies)
- Publication cost by county, 2026, vendor all-inclusive (newspapers, affidavits, $50 state fee): Putnam $685; Dutchess $425; Orange $425; Ulster $425; Columbia $425; Greene $425; Sullivan $445; Rockland $445; Westchester $395; Albany $395; New York County $1,795. "Each county clerk designates specific newspapers, and those newspapers set their own legal-notice rates." [CLAIMED, publication vendor] — [LLC Publishers 2026 county breakdown](https://www.llcpublishers.com/learn/how-much-does-llc-publication-cost-in-new-york-county-by-county-breakdown-2026); a second vendor shows wider ranges (Dutchess $200–600, Orange $100–525, Putnam $350–650, Ulster $150–400) [CLAIMED] — [URS Agents estimates](https://www.ursagents.com/new-york-publication-cost-estimates.html)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Because the LLC's publication county is the county of the LLC's office address, an office in Putnam costs the most to publish ($685) and one in Dutchess/Orange/Ulster the least (~$425); some founders use a registered-agent address in a cheap county, which the attorney should evaluate.
- [INFERENCE] Entity cost to launch: $200 (articles) + ~$425–$685 (publication incl. $50 certificate) + $25 (assumed name, if branding under a trade name) = roughly $650–$910, before legal drafting time; then $9 every two years.
- [INFERENCE] Licensing cost to launch for an attorney-broker LLC: $185 (LLC broker licence) + $185 (the attorney as member acting as broker, if DOS treats it as a separate licence per §441-b) = $370 per two-year term, plus $185 per branch office and $15 if an exam is required; renewal is $185 per licence every two years. Whether the attorney must sit the exam and take the 152 hours is unconfirmed (see Gaps), but §442-f means the attorney may act as broker without any licence; a licence is needed in practice to sponsor salespersons and to join the MLS/Realtor association, which require a licence number.
- [INFERENCE] The escrow rule means a separate federally insured, non-interest-retaining trust account from day one; most banks offer these at no or nominal cost, but I found no fee source.

### Gaps
- No DOS page I could reach states the exam/education/experience exemption for attorneys applying for a broker licence; the DOS FAQ only confirms the CE exemption and that attorney-status applications are filed on paper. The attorney should ask DOS Licensing (518-474-4429) which schedule and proof of admission apply.
- DOS does not publish expedited-handling fees on the LLC page, and I could not find current licence-processing times (the processing-times URL returned 404).
- Bank fees for the broker escrow account were not researched (no primary source).

## 3. Insurance: E&O, general liability, cyber; NY requirements

### Takeaway
New York does not mandate E&O for real-estate licensees, but MLSs, lenders and clients usually require it; a small NY brokerage should model roughly $700–$2,000 per year for $1M/$1M E&O (median quotes ~$708/year; revenue-rated firm policies $2,000–$3,000 per $1M of commission revenue), about $400–$700 per year for general liability or a BOP, and about $700–$1,550 per year for cyber; workers' compensation and disability become mandatory with the first employee.

### Cited Findings
- New York: E&O "does not require E&O coverage" by law but is usually contractually required; "New York law requires workers' comp for real estate businesses that have employees" (policy also provides NY disability benefits); commercial auto required for business-owned vehicles. Average monthly premiums for NY agents/brokers buying through Insureon: E&O $59, general liability $42, BOP $45. [PRIMARY, insurer marketplace] — [Insureon: NY real estate agent/broker insurance](https://www.insureon.com/real-estate-business-insurance/agents-brokers/new-york)
- Median policy costs for real-estate agents and brokers (TechInsurance): E&O $59/month ($708/year, $1M/$1M); general liability $33/month ($400/year, $1M/$2M); BOP $58/month ($692/year); cyber $58/month ($692/year); workers' comp $41/month ($489/year). [SECONDARY, insurer marketplace, undated] — [TechInsurance cost page](https://www.techinsurance.com/real-estate-insurance/agent-broker/cost)
- Small-business cyber insurance: "Small businesses pay an average of $129 per month, or $1,552 annually"; limits "typically range from $1 million to $5 million"; updated April 24, 2026. [PRIMARY, insurer marketplace] — [Insureon cyber cost](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/cyber-liability/cost)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] For a firm with under $200K GCI in year one, model E&O at ~$900–$1,500 (the revenue-rated $2,000–$3,000 per $1M implies $400–$600 at $200K GCI, but minimum premiums push small firms toward the $700–$1,500 band); scale to ~$1,500–$3,000 at $1M GCI.
- [INFERENCE] A sensible launch insurance budget: E&O $1,200 + BOP/GL $500 + cyber $1,000 = ~$2,700/year, rising with employees (workers' comp ~$500+/year per the median quote) and with any hired support staff; NY disability/PFL premiums are additional and were not researched.
- [INFERENCE] Because the model replaces agents with software and an attorney, the cyber policy may matter more than for a typical brokerage (client PII, wire instructions); insurers will also ask about wire-fraud controls.

### Gaps

- NY statutory disability/Paid Family Leave premium costs for employees were not researched.

## 9. Human time: broker hours per transaction under NY supervision duties, support staff, on-call attorney review; launch effort versus steady state

### Takeaway
NY law requires the broker to supervise salespersons with "regular, frequent and consistent personal guidance, instruction, oversight and superintendence", to accept every listing, keep transaction records and run the escrow account; with no salespersons the supervision burden is largely internal, so broker time per transaction is set by the work the software does not do — I estimate 8–15 broker hours per side with a mature system versus 25–40 hours for a conventional agent (unsourced estimate; NAR reports the typical agent closes only nine sides a year). Launch is gated by the six-week LLC publication, the DOS licence, HGAR processing (3–5 business days) and OneKey orientation (within 90 days), i.e. roughly 8–12 weeks of calendar time, during which the founders' effort is mostly software and legal set-up.

### Cited Findings
- Supervision standard: "regular, frequent and consistent personal guidance, instruction, oversight and superintendence" (19 NYCRR 175.21(a), implementing RPL §441(1)(d)); broker and salesperson must keep written records of listings and transactions; salesperson "active service under the supervision of a licensed real estate broker" of 35 hours/week for 50 weeks/year counts for experience. [PRIMARY] — [19 NYCRR 175.21](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/19-NYCRR-175.21)

[PRIMARY] — [DOS Broker FAQ](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-broker-frequently-asked-questions)
- Escrow deposits within three business days into a dedicated account (broker's personal duty). [PRIMARY] — [19 NYCRR 175.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/19-NYCRR-175.1)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Broker hours per side with the software doing intake, scheduling, document assembly and drafting: listing consult and pricing review 1–2 h; listing agreement, agency disclosure and PCDS review 0.5–1 h; MLS entry QA and photo approval 0.5 h; showing/feedback oversight 1 h; offer review and negotiation 2–3 h; contract-to-close coordination with both attorneys, inspector, appraiser and lender 2–4 h; closing 1 h → about 8–15 hours per side. For a buyer side add showings (3–6 h unless delegated to a licensed showing agent or self-showing lockbox). A conventional agent is commonly assumed to spend 25–40 hours per side; no reliable primary source for either number was found, so treat both as model assumptions.
- [INFERENCE] Capacity: at 10–12 hours per side and a 45-hour week, one broker can handle roughly 150–200 sides a year only if support staff absorb scheduling, document chasing and client communication; at 40 hours per side the ceiling is ~50 sides. NY law does not let unlicensed staff negotiate or show property, so the design must keep negotiation and listing acceptance with the broker.
- [INFERENCE] Support staff: one unlicensed coordinator at $20–$25/hour (above the $16 floor; BLS area wages could not be fetched) ≈ $42,000–$52,000/year plus ~10% payroll taxes and workers' comp (~$500/year), handling ~150–250 transactions.
- [INFERENCE] On-call attorney review: if the attorney-broker performs the brokerage's legal review himself, the cost is his time (0.5–1.5 h per side); if separate counsel is retained for independent review, flat fees of $995–$3,000 per side are the market rate — but note the attorney cannot represent both parties to the sale, and representing a client as attorney while taking a brokerage fee from the same transaction raises conflict rules the attorney must clear.
- [INFERENCE] Launch effort: entity and publication (6–8 weeks elapsed, ~10 hours), licence/MLS/association (2–4 weeks elapsed, ~10 hours), insurance (~5 hours), bank/escrow set-up (~3 hours), legal forms and disclosures (~40–80 attorney hours), software build (the son-in-law's full time for months, not costed here). Steady state: broker 10–15 h per side; coordinator ~2–4 h per side; marketing 5–10 h per week.

### G1. Licensing, entity and the attorney route

#### Takeaway
The attorney needs no course, experience or exam: DOS's own application says admitted attorneys are exempt, so his licence is a paper application and $185 every two years. A one-broker LLC pays one $185 fee, not two. A New York LLC also owes an annual IT-204-LL filing fee ($25 at under $100,000 of New York-source gross income, rising by bracket), which the notes above did not include.

#### Cited Findings
- "Attorneys who are admitted to the New York State bar are exempt from the educational, experience and examination requirements. Submit a completed application and fee, indicating on the application that you are admitted to the New York State bar." The nonrefundable fee is $185 and "the license will be effective for two years"; the qualification checkbox reads "Attorney Admitted to NYS Bar". Form DOS-0036-f-a (Rev. 09/23), pp. 1–3. [PRIMARY, accessed 2026-10-23] — [DOS broker application](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-broker-application)
- The same form: an LLC "member or manager of the limited liability company ... who meets the qualifications for licensure, may apply for a real estate license to conduct business as a real estate brokerage under the limited liability company" and applies "as the representative (principal) real estate broker" (p. 4). [PRIMARY, accessed 2026-10-23] — [DOS broker application](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-broker-application)
- RPL §441-b: broker fee "one hundred fifty-five dollars plus an additional thirty dollar surcharge"; the entity licence carries "the fee required by this section for the license to the corporation", and each *additional* officer, member or manager acting as broker pays the same fee; term "not less than two years" (revision 2022-08-19). [PRIMARY, accessed 2026-10-23] — [RPL §441-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/441-B)
- DOS fee page: "$185 - Initial Application", "$185 - Branch Office Application", "$185 - Renewal", "$15 - Written Exam". [PRIMARY, accessed 2026-10-23] — [DOS: Become a broker](https://dos.ny.gov/become-real-estate-broker)
- IT-204-LL (2025 instructions): an LLC with no New York-source gross income, or a disregarded single-member LLC with any New York income, pays $25; LLC fee table by prior-year New York-source gross income: up to $100,000 → $25; $100,000–$250,000 → $50; $250,000–$500,000 → $175; $500,000–$1,000,000 → $500; $1M–$5M → $1,500; $5M–$25M → $3,000; over $25M → $4,500; due the 15th day of the third month after year end; no extension. [PRIMARY via WS2 notes] — [RPL §441-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/441-A), [DOS Real Estate License Law (Jan. 2024)](https://dos.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2024/07/re-law.pdf)

### G3. Insurance: the brokerage needs its own policies, and the lawyer's policy will likely not help

#### Takeaway
Marketplace medians are reconfirmed: E&O about $708 a year for $1M/$1M, general liability about $400, a business owner's policy about $692, cyber $692–$1,552; New York does not require E&O.
