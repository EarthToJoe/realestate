Brief's exact ask: "Listing and data access: can software list a property on Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, and
the regional MLSs (e.g. OneKey MLS, Hudson Valley MLS)? Is there an API, or does a human have to click
through manually? What memberships or subscriptions are required and what do they cost?"

here" markers "until workstream 3 confirms what's possible, then wire up whatever can be automated".

Specific requirements:
- One row per channel in a channel table: OneKey MLS; the Hudson Valley MLS options that actually exist
  today (identify them by their current names, e.g. the Ulster/Columbia/Greene board's MLS, Mid-Hudson
  MLS/HGAR history, Dutchess/Orange/Sullivan coverage, any merger or shutdown dates); Zillow; Redfin;
  Realtor.com; Homes.com; plus any route that matters (syndication feeds such as ListHub, direct listing
  feeds, FSBO/"For Sale by Owner" uploads, Zillow's listing rules for off-MLS listings). Columns:
  `Channel | Can software create a listing? | Route (API / feed / web form / via MLS only) | Who may do it

  and AI rules | Verdict for the POC (automate / stub with human marker / skip)`.
- Separate listing INPUT (getting a listing onto a channel) from data OUTPUT (pulling listings for the buyer

- Cover the rules that bite a flat-fee, AI-led, one-broker firm: Clear Cooperation and its 2025 changes
  (Multiple Listing Options for Sellers, delayed marketing), Zillow's listing standards, limited-service or
  "entry-only" listing rules in OneKey and the other local MLS(s), lockbox and showing-service access
  (SentriLock, ShowingTime), photo and copy rights.
- Give the cheapest compliant path to get a Hudson Valley listing onto all major portals, step by step,

- Handoff traceability items: one line per channel (and per data feed) saying exactly what the POC does
  there: automated call, or a stub with a "human required here: <reason>" marker.
