# Your workstream: WS4, cost model

Brief's exact ask: "Cost model: what it costs to get our foot in the door (upfront), what the ongoing
subscription and service costs are, and what human interaction is required at launch versus ongoing."
Context from the brief: the pitch is a dramatically cheaper experience than 2.5 to 3% per side; Joe wants
to invest heavily; the plan must be sober and credible.

Notes file (yours to append to):
- `research_notes/WS4 Cost model/costs_and_revenue_benchmarks.md`

Deliverable: `docs/research/WS4-cost-model.md`
Handoff: `docs/research/handoff/WS4.md`

Specific requirements:
- Cross-check membership, MLS, licensing, insurance and inspection costs against the WS2, WS3 and WS6 notes
  (read-only); where they differ, use the newer primary source and say so.
- An upfront cost table and an ongoing cost table, each line: `Item | One-time / monthly / annual | Low |
  High | Required by (law / MLS rule / practical) | Source | Label | Date`. Include licensing and entity
  formation, broker licence and education if he needs it, Realtor and MLS dues, lockboxes, errors-and-
  omissions insurance, general liability, cyber insurance, e-signature, transaction management, CRM,
  telephony/SMS (10DLC registration), photography and media per listing, portal advertising, website and
  hosting, the AI model costs of the product itself (estimate per transaction from current Anthropic API
  pricing, label [INFERENCE] with the arithmetic shown), legal review of contract templates, accounting,
  and escrow account setup.
- Human interaction: hours of licensed-broker time and of unlicensed help per transaction, at launch versus
  steady state, by stage; tie to the functions that need a human (WS1) where the notes allow.
- Revenue side: plausible fee models (flat fee per side, low percentage, a-la-carte), with benchmarks from
  named competitors and NY/Hudson Valley median prices (dated). Show unit economics per transaction and the
  break-even number of closings per year under low, middle and high cost cases. Show the arithmetic.
- State plainly the fixed-cost total for year one, the variable cost per closing, the break-even volume,
  and which kill criteria (A7, K1, K2, K7) the numbers hit or dodge.
- Handoff traceability items: none needed beyond cost lines that drive POC features (e.g. a cost-per-deal
  tracker); put the key numbers the plan and the executive summary should quote in the handoff.
